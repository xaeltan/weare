Xael Tan has spent the past decade where faith, work and technology meet, exploring how they can come together to serve people well.

He leads Inspireo, which has built custom web and mobile apps since 2011 and now helps growing SMEs put the right systems in place: a Business OS for their data and workflows first, then AI tools and agents on top. The result is a business that connects its sales, operations and finance, and frees its people for work that needs human judgement.

As AI enters the workplace, Xael helps teams adopt it as part of a coordinated system rather than a set of separate tools. A well-structured foundation reduces noise, surfaces insight and supports wiser operations. His aim is for organisations to scale through trust, clarity and stewardship, and to need less heroics from the founder.

Since 2016, Xael has served with Indigitous, contributing to #HACK, a missional hackathon. There he has journeyed with teams exploring where innovation and mission meet, to form people who listen well, discern deeply and respond faithfully.

He recently completed a programme in Digital Transformation & Change Management with BCG, which sharpened how he guides change across people, process and systems.
